### ✦ Featured project — Konspekt Diktor

> **An open-source AI-powered study assistant.** Transform study materials into structured notes, listen to voice dictation, ask questions, and prepare for exams — right in your browser.

- 📄 Works with text, PDF, DOCX, and photos of study materials
- 🔊 Voice dictation, playback controls, and offline listening of prepared notes
- 🧠 AI questions, revision tools, and exam preparation
- 🌍 Russian and English interfaces
- 🔧 A JavaScript web app with a Cloudflare Worker backend and automated testing
